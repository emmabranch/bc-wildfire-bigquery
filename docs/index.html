Portfolio project · Google BigQuery · BigQuery GIS · R

Fewer fires. Far more burned.

76 fire seasons of BC Wildfire Service records, loaded into Google BigQuery and analysed with SQL and BigQuery GIS. The number of fires each season hasn’t gone up, but the area they burn has.

What the data show

  • Area burned has risen sharply while the number of fires has not. Annual fire counts since 2020 are within or below the historical range, but the average area burned each year is many times higher than in earlier decades.
  • 2023 was an extreme outlier. About 3.86M hectares burned, nearly three times the next largest seasons, 2018 and 2017.
  • Lightning drives most of the area burned. Lightning caused about half of all fires but 77% of area burned.
  • Area burned is concentrated in the north. The Prince George Fire Centre accounts for over half of all area burned since 1950.
  • Area burned near communities has increased. Within 50 km of Kamloops, more land burned from 2020 to 2025 than in any earlier decade.

This is an independent portfolio project using open BC Wildfire Service data. It is not an official BC Wildfire Service product, and it is not intended for legal, planning, or insurance decisions. See Methods and SQL for data sources, cleaning steps, and limitations.
